## 1. Architecture & Block Summary
- **MCU, Clock, VCAP_1 & VDDA Conditioning, External Button-Chord DFU Override, Bootloader Idle Conditioning & SWD Breakout (`U1`, `Y1`, `C1`, `C2`, `C10`, `L2`, `C21`, `C22`, `Q12`, `Q13`, `Q14`, `SW15`, `SW16`, `R25..R29`, `TP1..TP6`):**
  - `STM32F412RET6` (`U1`, LCSC `C89374`, LQFP-64): 100 MHz ARM Cortex-M4F (operated at `SYSCLK = 80 MHz` / `APB2 = 80 MHz`) with 512 KB Flash and 256 KB contiguous DMA-capable SRAM.
  - 12 MHz Low-$C_L$, Low-ESR HSE Crystal (`Y1`, `TXM12M0004322CBCDO00T`, LCSC `C362368`, SMD3225-4P): $C_L = 9\text{ pF}$, $\text{ESR}_{\max} = 60\ \Omega$, $C_0 \approx 3\text{ pF}$. Critical transconductance $g_{m,\text{crit}} = 4 \times 60\ \Omega \times (2\pi \times 12\text{ MHz})^2 \times (9\text{ pF} + 3\text{ pF})^2 \approx 0.20\text{–}0.25\text{ mA/V} \ll G_{m,\text{crit,max}} = 1.0\text{ mA/V}$ (gain margin $> 16$, compliant with ST AN2867 and STM32F412xE DS11189 Section 6.3.9). Load capacitors `C1 = C2 = 12 pF` 50 V C0G (`0402CG120J500NT`, LCSC `C1547`) provide $C_{L,\text{actual}} = (12 \times 12)/(12 + 12) + C_{\text{stray}}(3\text{ pF}) = 6\text{ pF} + 3\text{ pF} = 9.0\text{ pF}$.
  - Single-`VCAP_1` Core LDO Decoupling (`U1.30`): Because LQFP-64 bonds out a single internal core voltage regulator capacitor pin (`VCAP_1`, `U1.30`), `VCAP_1` is decoupled by a 4.7 uF 16 V 0603 X5R low-ESR ($\ll 1\ \Omega$) ceramic capacitor `C10` (`CL10A475KO8NNNC`, LCSC `C19666`), meeting ST DS11189 Table 21 and AN4488 ($1 \times 4.7\ \mu\text{F}$ nominal for single-VCAP packages).
  - Analog Supply Isolation (`U1.13` `VDDA/VREF+`): Isolated from digital `+3V3` via 0603 ferrite bead `L2` (`BLM18PG121SN1D`, LCSC `C14709`, $120\ \Omega$ @ 100 MHz, $R_{\text{DC}} = 50\text{ m}\Omega$, 2 A) feeding net `VDDA`, locally decoupled adjacent to `U1.13` (`VDDA`) and `U1.12` (`VSSA`) by `C21` = 100 nF (`CL05B104KB54PNC`, LCSC `C307331`) and `C22` = 1 uF (`CL05A105KO5NNNC`, LCSC `C29266`).
  - Factory-ROM USB DFU, External Button-Chord Recovery (`Select + R2 + B`), Reset & SWD Programming:
    - `BOOT0` (`U1.60`) has a 10 kOhm pull-down to `GND` (`R8`, LCSC `C25744`) for normal Flash boot (`BOOT1`/`PB2` pulled low via 10 kOhm `R11`).
    - For bare-PCBA bring-up prior to LCD installation, tactile switch `SW15` (`B3U-1000P`, LCSC `C231329`) and test pad `TP6` pull `BOOT0` to `+3V3`, tactile switch `SW16` (`B3U-1000P`) and `TP3` pull `NRST` (`U1.7`, with 10 kOhm pull-up `R7` and 100 nF filter `C8`) low, and `TP1..TP6` (`RH-5015`, LCSC `C5199798`) expose `SWDIO` (`PA13`), `SWCLK` (`PA14`), `NRST`, `+3V3`, `GND`, and `BOOT0`.
    - For sealed-enclosure recovery without opening the press-fit CNC shell, series P-MOSFETs `Q12` (`AO3401A`, Source on `+3V3`, Gate on `BTN_SELECT`, Drain on `BOOT0_CHORD_MID`) and `Q13` (`AO3401A`, Source on `BOOT0_CHORD_MID`, Gate on `BTN_R2`, Drain on `BOOT0`) pull `BOOT0` to `+3V3` (`3.30 V` across `R8 = 10 kOhm`) whenever external buttons `Select` (`SW9`) and `R2` (`SW13`) are held simultaneously. While `BOOT0 = 3.30 V` biases the gate of N-MOSFET `Q14` (`AO3400A`, Gate on `BOOT0`, Source on `BTN_B`, Drain on `NRST`), pressing and releasing external button `B` (`SW8`) pulls `NRST` low through `Q14` ($V_{\text{GS}} = 3.30\text{ V}$) and releases `NRST` with `BOOT0 = 1`, cleanly entering the ST System Memory ROM USB DFU bootloader from outside the enclosure. During normal gameplay (`BOOT0 = 0 V`), `Q14` has $V_{\text{GS}} = 0\text{ V}$ and its internal body diode (anode on `BTN_B`, cathode on `NRST`) is reverse-biased when `BTN_B` is pressed.
    - Dedicated 10 kOhm pull-ups to `+3V3` (`R25..R29`, plus `R14`/`R15` and button pull-ups `R33..R45`, `R19`) hold all active AN2606 system-memory bootloader pins idle high during DFU enumeration.
- **USB-C Power, CC1/CC2 ADC Current-Advertisement Sensing, Controlled TP4056 Charging & 0V Battery Wake / Reverse-Polarity Protection (`J1`, `F1`, `C3`, `D3`, `D4`, `U4`, `R1`, `R2`, `R52`, `R53`, `U2`, `R3`, `R51`, `F2`, `Q6`, `Q7`, `Q11`, `R20`, `R30`, `R31`, `Q1`, `D1`, `J3`, `R23`, `R24`):**
  - 16-pin USB-C receptacle `J1` (`TYPE-C-31-M-12`, LCSC `C165948`) configured as an autonomous 5 V sink with independent 5.1 kOhm Rd pull-down resistors (`R1`, `R2`, LCSC `C25905`) on `CC1` (`A5`) and `CC2` (`B5`), protected against ESD and overvoltage transients by a 5 V low-clamping bidirectional dual TVS diode `D4` (`PESD5V0S2BT`, LCSC `C2687130`, $V_{\text{RWM}} = 5.0\text{ V}$, $V_{\text{BR}} = 6.0\text{–}7.2\text{ V}$, $V_{\text{CL}} = 9\text{ V}$ typ at $I_{\text{PP}} = 1\text{ A}$, $12\text{ V}$ max at $I_{\text{PP}} = 3\text{ A}$).
  - USB-C Source Current Advertisement Sensing (`USB_CC1_ADC` on `U1.14` `PA0`/`ADC1_IN0`, `USB_CC2_ADC` on `U1.27` `PB1`/`ADC1_IN9`): `USB_CC1` and `USB_CC2` are routed through 10 kOhm 1% 0402 series current-limiting resistors `R52` and `R53` (`0402WGF1002TCE`, LCSC `C25744`) to `U1.14` (`PA0`/`ADC1_IN0`) and `U1.27` (`PB1`/`ADC1_IN9`). The total external ADC source impedance is $R_{\text{AIN}} = 5.1\text{ k}\Omega + 10\text{ k}\Omega = 15.1\text{ k}\Omega \le 50\text{ k}\Omega$ ($R_{\text{AIN,max}}$ per STM32F412 DS11189 Table 65), while `D4` (`PESD5V0S2BT`) + `R52`/`R53` (`10 kOhm`) limit pin injection current during any ESD/overvoltage event to $\le (12\text{ V} - 0\text{ V}) / 10\text{ k}\Omega = 1.2\text{ mA} \ll 5.0\text{ mA}$ ($|I_{\text{INJ(PIN)}}|$ rating in DS11189 Table 14), safely measuring the voltage across `R1` and `R2` to detect Default USB power ($0.25\text{–}0.61\text{ V}$, $0.41\text{ V}$ typ), 1.5 A Type-C source advertisement ($0.70\text{–}1.16\text{ V}$, $0.92\text{ V}$ typ), or 3.0 A Type-C source advertisement ($1.31\text{–}2.04\text{ V}$, $1.68\text{ V}$ typ).
  - Controlled `TP4056` (`U2`, LCSC `C725790`) Charger Enable & USB 2.0 Current Compliance (`U2.8` `CE` on `CHG_CE`, `U1.54` `PD2`, `R51`, `R3`):
    - `U2.8` (`CE`) is connected to `U1.54` (`PD2`) on net `CHG_CE` with a 100 kOhm pull-down resistor `R51` (`0402WGF1003TCE`, LCSC `C25741`) to `GND`. Upon USB plug-in, during reset, or prior to USB enumeration, `R51` holds `CHG_CE = 0 V` ($< 0.8\text{ V}$ `TP4056` shutdown threshold), keeping `U2` disabled ($I_{\text{CC}} \le 55\ \mu\text{A}$) while backlight MOSFET `Q2` is held off by `R10` = 100 kOhm. Total unconfigured `/VBUS` current is therefore only the `U3` + `U1` startup current ($\sim 40.6\text{ mA} \le 100\text{ mA}$ USB 2.0 unconfigured limit).
    - `R3` (`0402WGF3301TCE`, LCSC `C25890`, 3.3 kOhm 1%) programs `U2` charge current to $I_{\text{CHG}} = 1200\text{ V} / 3.3\text{ k}\Omega = 363.6\text{ mA}$ ($0.30\text{C}$ for the 1200 mAh cell, $P_{\text{diss,max}} \le 0.71\text{ W}$). Once USB enumeration completes (or 1.5 A / 3.0 A Type-C advertisement is detected on `PA0`/`PB1`), `U1` drives `PD2 = HIGH` (`3.30 V > 1.5 V` `TP4056` enable threshold). During simultaneous play-while-charging at 100% backlight duty cycle, total `/VBUS` current is $363.6\text{ mA (U2)} + 40.6\text{ mA (U3)} + 64.0\text{ mA (J2)} = 468.2\text{ mA}$ typ ($480.2\text{ mA}$ max at $V_{\text{BUS}} = 5.25\text{ V}$), strictly within the **500 mA USB 2.0 SDP limit**.
  - 0V PCM-Tripped Battery Wake & Reverse-Polarity Protection (`J3`, `F2`, `Q6`, `Q7`, `Q11`, `R20`, `R30`, `R31`):
    - Battery connector `J3` (Molex PicoBlade `53261-0271`, LCSC `C177225`) is protected immediately adjacent to `J3` by series PTC polyfuse `F2` (`BSMD0805-110-12V`, LCSC `C910824`) and P-MOSFET `Q6` (`AO3401A`, LCSC `C15127`, Source on `VBAT`, Drain on `VBAT_FUSED`, Gate on `VBAT_RP_G` with 100 kOhm source-gate pull-up `R20` `C25741` and pull-down N-MOSFET `Q7` `AO3400A` `C20917`).
    - `Q7` Gate (`VBAT_RP_EN`) is biased from `VSYS` through `R31` = 100 kOhm (`0402WGF1003TCE`, LCSC `C25741`) and clamped to `VBAT_FUSED` through `R30` = 10 kOhm (`0402WGF1002TCE`, LCSC `C25744`) in series with negative-polarity clamp N-MOSFET `Q11` (`AO3400A`, LCSC `C20917`, Gate on `GND`, Source on `VBAT_FUSED`, Drain on `VBAT_RP_CLAMP`):
      1. **0V PCM-Tripped Pack + USB Connected (`VBAT_FUSED = 0 V`, `VSYS = 4.70 V`):** `Q11` has $V_{\text{GS}} = 0\text{ V}$ (channel off) and its internal body diode (anode on `VBAT_FUSED`, cathode on `VBAT_RP_CLAMP`) is reverse-biased. `R31` pulls `VBAT_RP_EN` to `VSYS = 4.70 V`, turning `Q7` and `Q6` on so `U2` (`TP4056`) can deliver its $36.4\text{ mA}$ trickle-charge current into `J3.1` to reset the battery PCM and wake the pack.
      2. **Battery-Only Normal Operation (`VBAT_FUSED = +2.5 V..+4.2 V`, `VBUS = 0 V`):** `Q11`'s body diode conducts forward through `R30 = 10 kOhm` and `R31 = 100 kOhm` pulls from `VSYS`, biasing `VBAT_RP_EN` to $+2.5\text{–}4.2\text{ V}$ and keeping `Q7` and `Q6` fully enhanced ($V_{\text{GS}}(Q_6) = -V_{\text{BAT}}$).
      3. **Reverse Battery Insertion (`VBAT_FUSED = -1.5 V..-4.2 V`, with or without USB):** Because `Q11` Gate is at `GND` (`0 V`) and Source is at `VBAT_FUSED < -1.45 V`, `Q11` turns on hard ($V_{\text{GS}}(Q_{11}) = -V_{\text{BAT,FUSED}} = +1.5\text{ V..}+4.2\text{ V}$) and connects `R30` (`10 kOhm`) directly to negative `VBAT_FUSED`, pulling `VBAT_RP_EN` negative ($-0.91\text{ V}$ to $-3.37\text{ V}$ even if `VSYS = 4.95 V` via `R31 = 100 kOhm`), keeping `Q7` and `Q6` strictly off.
- **Hardware-Self-Latching Soft-Power Latch, DFU/Blank-MCU Hold, Manual-Reset Hold-Up, AC-Coupled VBUS Wake, 3.3 V Buck-Boost Regulation & Switched Battery Telemetry (`U3`, `L1`, `Q3`, `Q4`, `Q5`, `Q10`, `D5`, `R49`, `R50`, `C19`, `C20`, `C23`, `D2`, `Q8`, `Q9`, `R21`, `R22`, `C18`):**
  - Soft-power latch on `U3.6` (`EN`, net `BUCK_EN`): P-MOSFET `Q3` (`AO3401A`, LCSC `C15127`) with `C20` = 100 nF (`C307331`) across `VSYS`-to-`PWR_GATE` keeps $V_{\text{GS}}(Q_3) = 0\text{ V}$ during battery hot-plug.
  - Hardware Self-Latch (`D5` `BAT54C`, `Q10` `AO3401A`, `R49` = 100 kOhm `C25741`, `R18` = 560 kOhm `C132339`, `C19` = 1 uF `C29266`, `Q4` `AO3400A`): Biases `PWR_HOLD` to `2.61 V` in `68.8 ms` once `+3V3` rises, keeping `+3V3` active during blank-MCU bring-up, ROM USB DFU mode, and manual reset while allowing active-low shutdown via `PC6`.
  - `RT6150AGQW` (`U3`, LCSC `C147971`) with 2.2 uH inductor `L1` (`SWPA3015S2R2MT`, LCSC `C43389`) regulates `VSYS` (3.0–5.0 V) to `+3V3` (3.30 V, 800 mA rating).
  - `+3V3`-gated battery telemetry (`Q8`, `Q9`, `R32`, `R21 = R22 = 10 kOhm`, `C18 = 100 nF`) feeds `VBAT_ADC` on `U1.16` (`PA2` / `ADC1_IN2`).
- **Display & Backlight (`J2`, `Q2`, `R9`, `R46`, `R47`, `R48`):**
  - 18-pin 0.5 mm pitch bottom-contact horizontal FPC socket `J2` (`FH12-18S-0.5SH(55)`, LCSC `C506792`, `rot_deg = 180`) drives the 2.4-inch 320x240 18-pin SPI ILI9341 IPS TFT LCD (`Z240IT002` / `YX240QV29-T`) over 10.0 MHz 4-wire `SPI1` with `IM[2:0] = 110`.
  - Backlight anode `J2.12` (`LEDA`) is powered from `VSYS`, and cathodes `J2.2..5` (`LCD_LEDK1..LCD_LEDK4`) are driven through four individual **100 Ohm 1% 0603 (100 mW)** ballast resistors (`R9`, `R46`, `R47`, `R48`, `0603WAF1000T5E`, LCSC `C22775`) switched by `Q2` (`AO3400A`, LCSC `C20917`) on `PB0` (`TIM3_CH3`). At $V_{\text{SYS}} = 4.70\text{ V}$ typ ($4.95\text{ V}$ max on USB) and $V_F = 3.10\text{ V}$ typ ($3.00\text{ V}$ min), per-LED current is $16.0\text{ mA/LED}$ typ ($64.0\text{ mA}$ total) and $19.5\text{ mA/LED}$ max ($78.0\text{ mA}$ total, $P_{R} = 38\text{ mW} \ll 100\text{ mW}$), strictly within the ER-TFT024-3 15–20 mA/LED typical rating and $< 25\text{ mA/LED}$ absolute maximum DC rating.
